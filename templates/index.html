⚖️ AI & You: High School Academic Integrity Guide

Artificial intelligence is a powerful study partner when used transparently, but misusing it crosses academic honesty lines. Compare responsible vs. prohibited schoolwork habits below.

✅ Green Light: Ethical Study Partner

  • Concept Explainer: Asking AI to break down difficult AP Biology or Algebra concepts using analogies.
  • Practice Quiz Generator: Generating custom flashcards and mock test questions from your own class notes.
  • Brainstorming & Outlining: Getting topic ideas or structural feedback before writing your own draft.
  • Code Debugging Coach: Pasting an error message to understand why a loop failed without copying entire solutions.

🚫 Red Light: Academic Dishonesty

  • Ghostwriting Essays: Submitting AI-generated paragraphs or essays as your own original writing.
  • Bypassing Problem Solving: Copying homework answers directly without working through the steps.
  • Fabricating Citations: Using unverified AI-invented quotes or fake research sources in a bibliography.
  • Sharing Sensitive Data: Uploading classmates' personal info, test keys, or private school files to public bots.
